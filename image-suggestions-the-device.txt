Image suggestions for /the-device
=================================

In priority order — what would actually move the needle, not just decoration.


1. Hero — replacement for the current cabinet PNG
---------------------------------------------------
A clean studio product shot of the device against pure white, three-quarter
angle, with the camera lens visible on the front face.

OR — preferred for B2B —

A real in-cab photo: the device fitted to a fleet vehicle dashboard
(HGV / van / PSV) with hands holding the handset mid-test. Stronger for B2B
because it shows scale, context and "we're already running this."


2. "How drivers use it" (steps section) — a 4-frame photo strip
----------------------------------------------------------------
One photo per step:
  (a) Hands picking up the handset
  (b) Close-up of the driver exhaling into the device with the camera light on
  (c) Green PASS on the screen with the ignition turning
  (d) A phone or laptop showing the test event landing in the portal

Either real photography or a simple isometric illustration set.


3. "What's inside" — exploded-view diagram
-------------------------------------------
A semi-technical illustration showing the device split into its components —
fuel-cell sensor, camera lens, 4G modem, GPS module, ECU connector — with thin
callout lines.

Single SVG; cheap to produce; massively raises product credibility for
technical buyers (transport managers, HSE, fleet engineers).


4. "Installation" — three real photos in a row
-----------------------------------------------
  (a) An engineer in branded workwear arriving at a depot
  (b) The device being fitted under a vehicle dashboard with proper tools
      (not amateur splicing)
  (c) The finished cabin install — discreet, factory-grade

These do the most work for objection-handling: they prove the install isn't
a backyard job.


5. "What does it cost?" — portal screenshot mockup
---------------------------------------------------
A clean dashboard screenshot showing a few real-looking pilot rows
(driver name, vehicle, pass/fail, GPS pin, mini camera thumbnail).

A higher-fidelity version of just the test-events table would slot into the
cost section perfectly to support the pilot framing.


6. "Compatibility" — small drivetrain illustrations
----------------------------------------------------
Replace the icon chips (Petrol, Diesel, Battery-electric, Hybrid, HGV, PSV)
with small, clean two-tone illustrations of each vehicle type.

Tiny lift, low cost, makes the section less generic.


7. Optional — short looping product video
------------------------------------------
3-5 second silent loop of a driver testing on the device with the camera
capture happening — embedded as a <video autoplay muted loop> either above
the steps or replacing the hero figure.

Massively raises the perceived quality of the page if you can get one shot
well.


Quickest wins to commission first
---------------------------------
Items 1, 4 and 5. They're the highest-credibility shots for the priority B2B
buyer (fleet / ops / HSE) the marketing plan named.

The exploded-view diagram (3) and step strip (2) come next.

Chips (6) and video (7) are nice-to-haves.
