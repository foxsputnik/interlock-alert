§02

The Protocol

Four seconds between ignition
and accountability.

  1. i.

    Breathe

    Driver blows into the mouthpiece. Front camera captures an identity frame at the instant of the sample — no anonymous blowing.

    A
  2. ii.

    Analyse

    A clinical-grade fuel-cell sensor measures blood alcohol against the vehicle's pre-set threshold. Result returned in under three seconds.

    B
  3. iii.

    Permit or immobilise

    Below threshold: the ignition enables. Above it: the engine is immobilised at the ECU. No warning mode. No bypass.

    C
  4. iv.

    Log & transmit

    Every sample — pass, fail, retest — is stamped with driver photo, GPS and timestamp, and is in the portal inside ten seconds.

    D

§08

Questions of Fact

Frequently asked.

01. Is fitting an alcohol interlock legal in the UK?

Yes. Interlocks are fully legal for private, commercial and fleet use in the UK. They are installed by certified engineers and do not interfere with your vehicle's warranty or MOT.

02. Which vehicles is it compatible with?

Interlock Alert supports petrol, diesel, hybrid and pure-electric vehicles, including CAN-bus and keyless-start platforms. If it's road-legal in the UK, we install on it.

03. What happens if a driver fails a test?

The engine is immobilised at the ECU. The vehicle will not start. The failed event is logged with a driver photo, GPS location and timestamp, and an alert is sent to your nominated contacts by SMS and email.

04. Can the device be bypassed?

No. The driver-ID camera verifies who blew the sample, and a random rolling retest prevents a vehicle from being started sober and then handed over. Any tamper attempt is logged and flagged.

05. Who has access to the test data?

You do. Data is stored on UK-hosted infrastructure under your account, with granular role-based access for managers, admins and auditors. We never sell, resell or share your data.
